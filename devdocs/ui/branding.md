# Flint — Branding & Style Reference

Brand language: **Sharp. Grounded. Precise. Warm spark. Minimal. Confident.**
Not: Corporate. Bubbly. Over-engineered. Neon. Cold/clinical.

Reference aesthetics: Linear, Raycast, Vercel.

---

## Product Identity

- **Product name:** Flint
- **Tagline:** Light up your data.
- **Category:** B2B SaaS / Data intelligence platform

### Name & concept
The name comes from the primitive tool: strike flint against stone and you get fire. Flint takes raw, inert data structure and produces something that illuminates. The brand contrast — dark stone background, single warm spark accent — is the whole visual identity.

---

## Color Palette

Dark-mode-first. Two or three colors max: dark stone, warm spark, neutral.

| Role                  | Hex       | Usage                                      |
|-----------------------|-----------|--------------------------------------------|
| Primary background    | `#0D0D0D` | Page/app background                        |
| Secondary background  | `#1A1A1A` | Elevated surfaces, sidebars                |
| Tertiary background   | `#2A2A2A` | Cards, modals, dropdowns                   |
| Primary text          | `#E6E6E6` | High-contrast body and heading text        |
| Secondary text        | `#9A9A9A` | Lower-emphasis labels, metadata            |
| Tertiary text         | `#6A6A6A` | Lowest-emphasis, placeholders              |
| Primary orange        | `#FF4F00` | Primary actions, links, active states      |
| Orange hover          | `#E64700` | Hover state for primary orange elements    |
| Success green         | `#00BA88` | Positive states, confirmations             |
| Warning yellow        | `#FBBF24` | Caution states, approaching deadlines      |
| Error red             | `#EF4444` | Critical issues, destructive actions       |
| Border subtle         | `#2A2A2A` | Default borders, dividers                  |
| Border emphasis       | `#3A3A3A` | Focused/active borders                     |

## Typography

Dark-mode-first. Clean, modern sans-serif with slightly squared or geometric letterforms.

| Usage              | Font suggestion      | Weight           |
|--------------------|----------------------|------------------|
| Wordmark (FLINT)   | Geist or Satoshi     | Bold or Black    |
| Tagline            | Geist or Inter       | Medium or Regular|
| UI / Body text     | Geist or Inter       | Regular          |

---

## Logo Direction

Wordmark or simple icon + wordmark. The icon, if used, should evoke:
- A spark or single point of ignition (not a full flame — too generic)
- A flint stone edge — angular, sharp, geometric
- A single struck light — like a match catch, or a glint

The "F" in Flint is a natural anchor. The icon can live inside it or stand independently beside the wordmark.

**Avoid:** Literal database/table iconography (cylinders, grids, charts). Flint is about intelligence, not storage.

---

## Light Mode

- Background: Off-white or warm gray (not pure white)
- Text: Dark slate
- Accent: Same primary orange (`#FF4F00`) — warm against a light surface

---

## Voice & Tone

- Precise, not verbose
- Grounded, not salesy
- Approachable for a solo dev, credible for an engineering team
- "Light up your data" — the spark is the metaphor, not the feature
